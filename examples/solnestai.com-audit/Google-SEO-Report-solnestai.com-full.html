2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 40/100

What Works

  • HTTPS enforced, clean 200 responses across all eight sitemap URLs
  • robots.txt present, valid, and explicitly permissive to AI search crawlers
  • sitemap.xml present and valid at /sitemap.xml with 8 URLs
  • meta robots is index, follow with no X-Robots-Tag override

Findings

Six of eight pages canonicalise to the homepage Critical

/about, /services, /work, /book and /webinar all emit <link rel="canonical" href="https://solnestai.com">. Verified directly against raw HTML, not through a parser. A canonical pointing at a different URL tells Google the page is a duplicate and should not be indexed in its own right. These are not duplicates: /work alone holds 2,789 words of unique portfolio content.

Recommendation: Emit a self-referencing canonical on every page. /about should declare https://solnestai.com/about, and so on. This is almost certainly one shared layout component hardcoding the site root.

Core Web Vitals could not be measured Info

PageSpeed Insights returned a rate-limit error because no API key is configured. No field or lab data was collected, so no CWV claim is made in this report.

Recommendation: Run /solnest-seo:seo google setup to add a free Google API key, then re-run for real CrUX field data.

2.2 On-Page SEO

Score: 38/100

What Works

  • Every page has exactly one H1
  • Heading hierarchy is clean, with 24 H3s under 8 H2s on the homepage
  • Homepage carries 17 internal links and a coherent structure

Findings

Five pages share an identical title tag and meta description Critical

/, /about, /services, /work and /book all use the title "Solnest AI - Watch What Happens When AI Meets Your Business" and the same meta description verbatim. Duplicate titles across a small site are a strong duplicate-content signal and waste the highest-value on-page ranking element on four pages.

Recommendation: Write a unique, intent-matched title and description per page. /services should target the service query, /work the proof query, /about the entity query.

Duplicated brand suffix in the /webinar title Medium

The title reads "Live Demo - From Zero to Automated | Solnest AI | Solnest AI", repeating the brand twice. This is the signature of a template appending a suffix to a title that already contains one.

Recommendation: Strip the brand from the page-level title or make the template suffix conditional.

2.3 Content Quality

What Works

  • The blog post is a genuine 947-word comparison piece with 4 schema blocks
  • Copy is specific about verticals: short-term rentals, real estate, med spas

Findings

Four pages are thin High

/book is 52 words, /blog is 59, /about is 292 and /webinar is 317. /about at 292 words is well short of what an entity-establishing page needs, and it is one of the pages AI systems read to decide who a company is.

Recommendation: Prioritise /about and /services. Both are commercially important and both are currently too thin to rank or be cited.

2.4 Schema and Structured Data

Score: 85/100

What Works

  • Organization and ProfessionalService JSON-LD present on every page
  • Correct use of @id anchors such as #organization and #service
  • Blog post carries 4 schema blocks including article-level markup
  • Organization name is byte-identical to every other brand surface

Findings

Schema is duplicated rather than page-specific Low

The same Organization and ProfessionalService pair appears on all pages. That is valid and harmless, but page-type schema is missing where it would earn rich results.

Recommendation: Add Service schema on /services and FAQPage where genuine Q&A exists. Note this is a rich-results play, not an AI-citation lever.

2.5 Images

Score: 70/100

What Works

  • Zero missing alt attributes across all 8 pages, which is unusual and good

Findings

Six homepage images lack width and height Medium

6 of 9 homepage images declare no intrinsic dimensions, which allows layout shift while they load and harms Cumulative Layout Shift.

Recommendation: Add explicit width and height, or use the Next.js Image component which sets them automatically.

Only one image is lazy-loaded Low

1 of 9 homepage images uses loading="lazy".

Recommendation: Lazy-load everything below the fold, keeping the LCP image eager.

2.6 AI Search Readiness

Score: 55/100

What Works

  • All four search crawlers allowed in robots.txt and returning 200 at the CDN edge: OAI-SearchBot (ChatGPT), Claude-SearchBot (Claude), PerplexityBot (Perplexity), Googlebot (Google AI Overviews)
  • robots.txt correctly separates search crawlers from training crawlers, which most sites get wrong. OAI-SearchBot and Claude-SearchBot control citation; GPTBot and ClaudeBot are training only; Google-Extended has no effect on AI Overviews
  • Snippet-eligible: index, follow with no X-Robots-Tag override
  • Brand entity is byte-identical across all 5 machine-readable surfaces: title, og:site_name, jsonld:Organization, jsonld:ProfessionalService, llms.txt
  • AI visibility band: Readable

Findings

Five pages are canonicalised to the homepage, blocking AI retrieval Critical

Google AI Overviews and AI Mode retrieve from the ordinary Search index. /about, /services, /work, /book and /webinar all declare https://solnestai.com as canonical, so they are not eligible to be retrieved or cited. Verified against raw HTML.

Recommendation: Emit self-referencing canonicals. This is the single highest-leverage fix for both search and AI visibility.

Nothing on the site is quotable High

Main-content extraction yields 479 words from 1,921. The longest paragraph is 37 words against a 134 to 167 word target, with zero paragraphs over 40 words. Extractable text contains 0 percentages and 0 dollar figures. A model can read the page and has nothing to lift and attribute.

Recommendation: Add two or three genuine 130 to 160 word answer blocks with real numbers. Published pricing is the most quotable asset available and is absent from extractable text.

Only two of eight homepage H2s are question-form Medium

Question-shaped headings match how people phrase prompts and how retrieval segments a page.

Recommendation: Convert several H2s to the questions buyers actually ask.

Citability measurements Info

Extraction yields 479 of 1921 words (25%). Longest paragraph 37 words against a 134-167 target. Paragraphs over 40 words: 0. Percentages: 0. Dollar figures: 0. Question-form H2s: 2/8.

Recommendation: Track these on re-audit. They are the concrete measures behind the citability score.
